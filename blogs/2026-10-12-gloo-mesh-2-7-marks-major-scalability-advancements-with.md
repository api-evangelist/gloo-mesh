Gloo Mesh 2.7 introduces massive multi-cluster scalability, supporting up to 100 million pods, along with a redesigned user experience for improved observability and troubleshooting. With production support for Istio v1.24 and upcoming support for v1.25, this release enhances Istio Ambient mode, introduces peer-based multi-cluster networking, and tightens integration with Gloo Gateway. Explore how Gloo Mesh simplifies service mesh operations at scale.
